# Privacy Policy

**Pup Portrait** is operated by **Monomoy Strategies LLC** (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use Pup Portrait at [https://pup-portrait.com](https://pup-portrait.com) and related apps (the “Service”).

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026  
**Contact:** support@pup-portrait.com

## Information we collect

We collect information you provide and information created when you use the Service:

- **Account information.** Email address and password when you create an account. If you use a sign-in provider, we receive the identifier that provider shares with us.
- **Photos you upload.** Images of your dog that you choose to upload so we can generate portraits.
- **Generated portraits.** The portraits and scene variations we create for you, including prompts, breed or theme selections, and the resulting images.
- **Usage information.** How many free or paid generations you have used, subscription or pack status, and basic technical logs needed to run the Service.
- **Payment information.** If you buy a pack or subscription, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number. Stripe may provide us with a customer id, payment status, and the plan you purchased.
- **Device and browser information.** Standard request data such as browser type, device type, and approximate location derived from IP address, used to operate and secure the Service.

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Generate portraits and scene variations, including watermarked free-offer images
- Save portraits to your gallery when you have an account
- Enforce the one-time free offer (1 watermarked portrait + 5 scenes) and paid plan limits
- Process payments and prevent abuse
- Respond to support requests
- Improve reliability and safety of the Service

## Photos, AI generation, and sharing

When you upload a photo or request a portrait, we send the image and your generation request to our infrastructure (Supabase) and to the image-generation provider we use (Google Gemini / Nano Banana) so the portrait can be created.

Uploaded photos and generated images are stored so you can view them in your gallery and so we can deliver the Service you asked for. Free-tier images include a watermark.

We do not sell your photos. We do not use your uploaded photos to advertise to third parties.

## How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and file storage
- **Google** — AI image generation
- **Stripe** — payment processing
- **Service providers** who help us host, secure, or support the Service, under obligations to protect your information
- **Legal requests** when required by law, or to protect the Service, our users, or Monomoy Strategies LLC

## Retention

We keep account data, uploaded photos, and generated portraits while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of your account and associated photos.

## Your choices

- You may access or update account information by signing in.
- You may request deletion of your account and stored images by emailing support@pup-portrait.com.
- You may stop uploading photos at any time. Uploading a photo is optional; you can also generate from a selected breed.
- Payment methods are managed through Stripe.

## Children

Pup Portrait is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact support@pup-portrait.com and we will delete it.

## Security

We use industry-standard safeguards provided by our hosting, auth, and payment providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
